Common questions

How does a trip get built from photos?

Tap +, pick the photos from a trip, and choose where you started. Waymark reads when and where each photo was taken, groups nearby photos into stops, puts them in order and draws the driving route between them. You can rename, move, add or remove stops afterward.

Some of my photos didn't become stops.

A photo needs a location and a date to shape the route. Screenshots, photos saved from messages and photos taken with Location Services off for the Camera usually don't have one. Those photos are still saved with the trip; you can add the stop by hand with + stop.

How do states count?

A state counts when one of your stops is in it or a driving route passes through it. Flights don't count, so you can't collect Kansas from 35,000 feet. Counties and the top-100 cities work the same way.

One leg of my trip was a flight.

Waymark usually spots flights on its own, when two stops are too far apart to drive in the time between photos. If it guesses wrong, open the trip, tap edit route, and switch that leg between driving and flying. Flights go airport to airport, and the drives to and from the airports count.

A route took the wrong road.

Open the trip and tap edit route. You can pick a different route, avoid highways or tolls, or tap the map to send the route through a particular road.

Where is my data, and how do I move it to a new phone?

Everything stays on your iPhone. There's no account. Waymark's data is included in your iPhone's backup (iCloud Backup or a backup on your computer), so restoring a new phone from that backup brings your trips with it. See the privacy policy for details.

How do I unlock the other app icons?

Drive. "Ink" unlocks at 10 states and "Night Drive" at 10,000 miles. Switch icons from the app icon row on the stats tab.

Is it only for the US?

For now, yes. States, counties, cities and national parks are all US-based, so stops outside the US don't add to the counts.
