waymark: so far.

Privacy policy

Your trips stay yours.

Effective November 23, 2026

The short version: Waymark: so far. doesn't collect any data. There's no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your trips, photos and places are stored on your iPhone, and we never see them.

Who we are

Waymark: so far. ("Waymark," "the app") is made by Nathan Valentine, an independent developer. In this policy, "we" means the developer.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no server, no account and no sign-in. We don't collect, store, sell or share any personal information, and we can't see anything you put in the app.

What stays on your phone

Everything you add to Waymark is saved only on your iPhone:

  • Trips, stops, routes, places and notes that you create.
  • Photos you choose. When you add photos, you pick them with Apple's standard photo picker, so the app only sees the photos you select and never has access to the rest of your library. The app reads the date and location stored inside those photos to lay out your stops, and keeps its own smaller copy of each one so your map keeps working if you delete the original.
  • A few settings, such as whether sounds are on, your app icon, your last starting location and your last share message.

Like other app data, this information is included in your iPhone's own backups (iCloud Backup or a backup on your computer) if you have backups turned on. Those backups are handled by Apple under your Apple Account, not by us.

Apple Maps

To draw driving routes, search for places, name your stops and suggest places near your trip, the app asks Apple Maps. To do that, it sends Apple the map coordinates involved, such as your stops and the spots where your photos were taken. These are the same kind of requests any app makes when it shows a map or directions. Nothing else is sent: not your photos, trip names or notes. Apple handles these requests under Apple's privacy policy.

National parks, states, counties, cities and airports are checked on your phone against data built into the app, so those lookups don't leave your device.

Permissions

Waymark doesn't ask for access to your location, camera, microphone, contacts or full photo library. Photos come only from the ones you pick. The fonts and sounds are built into the app, so it doesn't download anything in the background.

Sharing

When you share a trip receipt or another image, you choose where it goes using the iPhone's share sheet. What you share is up to you and is handled by the app you send it to.

Deleting your data

You can delete any trip, photo or place in the app. Deleting the app removes everything it stored on your iPhone. Because we never receive your data, there's nothing for us to delete on our end.

Children

Waymark isn't directed at children under 13, and it doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app changes how data is handled (for example, an optional feature that needs a new permission), we'll update this page and the date at the top before that version is released.
